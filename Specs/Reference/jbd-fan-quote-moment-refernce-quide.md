# The Stormlight Archive: A Fan's Quote & Moment Reference Guide

## TL;DR
- The undisputed "big hitters" cited across every source are the **First Ideal / Immortal Words** ("Life before death, strength before weakness, journey before destination"), **Kaladin's Second and Third Windrunner Ideals** ("I will protect those who cannot protect themselves" / "I will protect even those I hate, so long as it is right"), and **Dalinar's "You cannot have my pain" → "I am Unity"** confrontation with Odium — these recur again and again on Goodreads, Coppermind, 17th Shard, TV Tropes, and roundup blogs.
- For exact original wording, the two best destinations are the **Coppermind wiki** (coppermind.net — per-character "/Quotes" pages plus the "Knights Radiant/Immortal Words" page, which carries per-book, per-chapter citations) and **Goodreads** (per-book "work/quotes" pages and per-character quote-tag pages, whose like-counts double as a popularity gauge); 17th Shard forums and TV Tropes' "Best Quotes for The Stormlight Archive" are best for fan-consensus favorites.
- This guide is organized by character, flags universally-beloved lines versus niche favorites, and points to where each can be verified — deliberately describing rather than reproducing long passages.

## Key Findings

**The "big hitters" (referenced repeatedly across multiple independent sources):**
1. The First Ideal / Immortal Words (all Radiants)
2. Kaladin's oath escalation, especially the Third Ideal spoken at the *Words of Radiance* climax
3. Dalinar's "You cannot have my pain" and "I am Unity" (*Oathbringer* ch. 119)
4. Dalinar's "The most important step... is always the next one" and "I will do better"
5. Szeth's opening line of the entire series ("...wore white on the day he was to kill a king")
6. "Journey before destination, you bastard" (Navani, echoing a Bridge Four refrain — a recurring fan-meme line)

**More niche but frequently-quoted:** Wit's storytelling/humor and "you will be warm again" speech; Jasnah's atheism and "boots" exchanges; Shallan's "I am Shallan / I am broken" Truths; Lift's comedic "awesomeness"; Rock's "airsick lowlanders"; Lopen's one-armed-cousin jokes.

**Best verification destinations by type:**
- **Oaths/Ideals structure:** coppermind.net/wiki/Knights_Radiant/Immortal_Words and the per-order pages (Order of Windrunners, Order of Skybreakers, etc.)
- **Per-character quotes:** Goodreads quote tags (goodreads.com/quotes/tag/kaladin, /hoid, /jasnah-kholin, /shallan-davar, /syl, /the-lopen, /szeth, /moash) and Coppermind "/Quotes" subpages (Hoid/Quotes, Lopen/Quotes, Jasnah_Kholin/Quotes)
- **Per-book quotes:** Goodreads "work/quotes" pages for each novel; Bookroo's "20 Best Words of Radiance Quotes"
- **Fan-consensus favorites:** TV Tropes "Best Quotes for The Stormlight Archive"; 17th Shard threads ("Favorite Quotes from Stormlight 1-5," "The Lopen funny Quotes," "Favorite Hoid/Wit quotes and scenes")

## Details (by character)

### The Knights Radiant Ideals / Immortal Words — core to the app's theming
- **The First Ideal** ("Life before death, strength before weakness, journey before destination") is shared by all ten orders and is the Radiants' motto. It is first taught in-story by **Teft** to Kaladin in *The Way of Kings*, and Kaladin whispers it himself shortly after (TWoK: "...Journey before destination. The First Ideal of the Knights Radiant."). Teft's fuller gloss — that the Radiant defends life always, that "living is harder than dying," and that strength "makes one capable of service" not rule — is a beloved expansion.
- **Structure:** After the shared First Ideal, each order has four further Ideals unique to it (Second through Fifth). Lightweavers are the exception — they have only the First Ideal and then progress by speaking personal Truths. Skybreakers, Elsecallers, and Lightweavers have the most individualized oaths; Brandon has noted (Fandom "Immortal Words" page) that "strength before weakness" is meant to be reinterpreted across the books (explored further in *Rhythm of War*).
- **Windrunner oaths (Kaladin's order):** Second — "I will protect those who cannot protect themselves"; Third — "I will protect even those I hate, so long as it is right"; Fourth — "I accept that there will be those I cannot protect."
- **Bondsmith Third Ideal (Dalinar):** "I will take responsibility for what I have done. If I must fall, I will rise each time a better man."
- **Skybreaker Ideals (Szeth's order), by name:** First (Ideal of Radiance), Second (Ideal of Justice), Third (Ideal of Dedication), Fourth (Ideal of Crusade), Fifth (Ideal of Law).
- **Where to find it:** coppermind.net/wiki/Knights_Radiant/Immortal_Words (per-order breakdown with book/chapter citations, e.g., Windrunner Third Ideal → *WoR* ch. 84; Bondsmith Third → *Oathbringer* ch. 119/121); the Fandom "Immortal Words" page for Brandon's interpretation notes. **Universally beloved** — the First Ideal is the single most-quoted phrase in the fandom.

### Kaladin Stormblessed (Windrunner) — the emotional core, most-quoted character
- **"Honor is dead. But I'll see what I can do."** (*Words of Radiance*) — actually a duet: Syl says "Honor is dead," Kaladin finishes it. Repeatedly named a favorite on 17th Shard and Goodreads. **Big hitter.**
- **Third Ideal at the WoR climax** — spoken through bloody lips as he stops Moash and Graves from killing King Elhokar, summoning Syl as a Shardblade for the first time. One of the most-cited scenes in the series (Goodreads tags /kaladin, /syl, /moash all anchor on this passage). **Big hitter.**
- **The sky-claim to Szeth** — "...but the sky and the winds are mine. I claim them, as I now claim your life" (*WoR*). Fans call it his most badass line.
- **Fourth Ideal** — "I accept that there will be those I cannot protect" (*Rhythm of War* climax, ~92%), spoken in the Tien vision after Teft's death; unlocks his Shardplate and lets him save his father. **Big hitter** for RoW-era fans.
- **"I'm broken." / "That's what they all were, silly."** — the Kaladin/Syl chasm exchange (*WoR*), a beloved mental-health-theme moment.
- **Wind and Truth arc:** Kaladin becomes the "Herald of Second Chances"; fans cite his reframed vow to protect himself so he can keep protecting others, and the humorous "I'm his therapist" beat regarding Szeth.
- **Where to find it:** goodreads.com/quotes/tag/kaladin; bookroo.com "13 Best Kaladin Quotes"; Coppermind Kaladin page.

### Dalinar Kholin (Bondsmith) — the philosophical heavyweight
- **"You cannot have my pain" → "I am Unity"** (*Oathbringer* ch. 119, Thaylen City) — refuses Odium, owns his atrocities at Rathalas, and forges Honor's Perpendicularity. The most-quoted Dalinar passage bar none; the full exchange is a heavily-liked standalone Goodreads quote (#10178823). **Biggest hitter.**
- **"The most important step a person can take is always the next one."** (*Oathbringer*) — endlessly inspirational-quote-listed.
- **"The most important words a man can say are, 'I will do better.'"** (*Oathbringer*) — cited with its qualifier that these aren't the most important words *any* man can say, but the ones *he* needed to say.
- **"Journey before destination. It cannot be a journey if it doesn't have a beginning"** and his Third Ideal, **"If I must fall, I will rise each time a better man."**
- **"What is a man's life worth?" / "A life is priceless"** exchange with Kaladin (*The Way of Kings*) — the "twenty-six hundred priceless lives"/Shardblade scene, deeply beloved (Goodreads /stormlight-archive tag).
- **Wind and Truth:** "Life breaks us. Then we fill the cracks with something stronger" (~5%); his capstone argument that keeping an oath is only as good as the ideals it's sworn to; Dalinar dies sacrificing himself ("Honor was born again in Dalinar Kholin"). His death is the most emotionally divisive event of Book 5.
- **Where to find it:** goodreads.com/quotes/tag/stormlight-archive and goodreads.com/work/quotes/23840254-oathbringer; the "You cannot have my pain" passage at Goodreads quote #10178823; littafi.com "Top 10 Dalinar Kholin Quotes."

### Shallan Davar (Lightweaver) — psychologically rawest
- **"I am Shallan / I am broken" and her Lightweaver Truths** — as a Lightweaver she advances not by fixed oaths but by speaking hard personal truths; her admissions about her past (that she killed her mother, and later her father) are central to her arc. **Big hitter** among character-arc fans.
- **The Veil / Radiant / Shallan identity split** — "That's the one I am. He knows" (*Oathbringer*, Adolin recognizing the real Shallan) is beloved; also "They're both equally false."
- **Jasnah's essay she reads** — "There is no role for women — there is, instead, a role for each woman, and she must make it for herself" (Jasnah's writing, quoted in Shallan's chapters).
- **Humor:** "I am a stick" (the Pattern/"you could be fire" chasm bit, *WoR*); "I had a splinter once. It eventually got out of hand" (*Oathbringer*).
- **Where to find it:** goodreads.com/quotes/tag/shallan-davar; Coppermind Shallan page.

### Szeth-son-son-Vallano (Skybreaker) — the series' bookend voice
- **The series' opening line** — "Szeth-son-son-Vallano, Truthless of Shinovar, wore white on the day he was to kill a king," followed by the "White to be bold... he was entitled to see you coming" reflection (*The Way of Kings* prologue). One of the most famous opening lines in modern fantasy; fans cite it constantly. The verbatim text is on Brandon's official site (brandonsanderson.com — "The Way of Kings Prologue") and as Goodreads quote #6845195. **Big hitter.**
- **"They named me Truthless!"** (*WoR*) — his anguished identity crisis when Kaladin reveals he too is a Surgebinder.
- **Oath progression (Skybreaker):** First, Second (Ideal of Justice), and Third (Ideal of Dedication — "I swear to follow the will of Dalinar Kholin") are sworn in *Oathbringer* (ch. 92 and 121); his Fourth Ideal (Ideal of Crusade — cleansing Shinovar of its false leaders) is undertaken and completed across *Wind and Truth*; his **Fifth Ideal (Ideal of Law)** is sworn in *Wind and Truth* (~ch. 133) — the "I make my own choices. I. Am. THE LAW!" oath — making him only the second Skybreaker after the Herald Nale to reach it, which frees him from the Third Ideal's binding to Dalinar's will.
- **Wind and Truth:** Szeth is the flashback POV character of Book 5; his break from his father's ghost ("Live and do better") and his minimalist creed — "I exist. I do what is needed... That is enough" — are widely highlighted.
- **Where to find it:** coppermind.net/wiki/Order_of_Skybreakers and coppermind.net/wiki/Szeth (authoritative for exact Ideal wordings and book/chapter); goodreads.com/quotes/tag/szeth; TV Tropes lists the Third and Fifth Ideal wordings.

### Jasnah Kholin (Elsecaller) — the scholar-queen, wit and steel
- **The "boots" scene** — Jasnah stages/manipulates a robbery in an alley to teach economics and philosophy, ending with Shallan told to hand over her boots. Fans use "boots" as shorthand. **Big hitter** for Jasnah fans.
- **"Atheism is not a disease... It's not as if I've caught a foot rash."** (*WoR*) — her signature retort.
- **"Shallan's mental image of Jasnah Kholin was of someone almost divine... an odd way to regard a determined atheist"** (*WoR*) — one of the most-liked Jasnah-tagged quotes.
- **"Control is the basis of all true power. Authority and strength are matters of perception."**
- **"Ignorance is hardly unusual, Miss Davar. The longer I live, the more I come to realize that it is the natural state of the human mind."**
- **Where to find it:** goodreads.com/quotes/tag/jasnah-kholin; coppermind.net/wiki/Jasnah_Kholin/Quotes (has book/chapter citations, e.g., TWoK ch. 5/29/36/48).

### Wit / Hoid — the fandom's favorite for humor + hidden depth
- **"I've abandoned my real name... you may call me Hoid"** (*The Way of Kings*) — his identity reveal to Kaladin.
- **The storyteller philosophy** — "The purpose of a storyteller is not to tell you how to think, but to give you questions to think upon" and "A story doesn't live until it is imagined in someone's mind" (*WoR*). **Big hitter** among Wit quotes.
- **"You will be warm again"** — the "it will get worse, then better... there will be sunshine again... I promise you, Kaladin" speech (*WoR*), a beloved depression/hope passage. **Big hitter.**
- **The Wandersail / "Dog and Dragon" parables** — his stories to Kaladin (esp. in *RoW*: "any meaning is for you to assign") are among the most emotionally cited.
- **Humor:** "Dumb luck... I'm lucky you're all so dumb"; "I like visiting people in prison. I can say whatever I want to them"; "the sound the world makes when it pisses itself" (*WoK*).
- **Where to find it:** coppermind.net/wiki/Hoid/Quotes; goodreads.com/quotes/tag/hoid; 17th Shard "Favorite Hoid/Wit quotes and scenes" thread; snugfam.com "70 Best Wit Quotes."

### Adolin Kholin — the beloved "ordinary" man
- **Killing Sadeas** — "My father thinks I'm a better man than he is. Unfortunately for you, he's wrong" (*WoR*, the alley murder). A defining, endlessly-discussed moment.
- **The prison/cologne banter with Kaladin** — "I'm refined, you insolent farmer" / "Poor boy" (*WoR*), one of the most-quoted comedic exchanges in the series. **Big hitter** for humor.
- **The Maya / Shardblade arc** — reviving his "dead" Blade Maya (Mayalaran) through kindness, peaking in *RoW* and *Wind and Truth*.
- **Wind and Truth:** "He was Adolin Kholin. A man with very good friends" — a fan-favorite affirmation of the non-Radiant hero; his leg wound and defense of Azir are the book's most-praised heroic arc.
- **Where to find it:** goodreads.com/quotes/tag/adolin-kholin; Coppermind Adolin page.

### Renarin Kholin (Truthwatcher) — the quiet fan-favorite
- Beloved more for character than one-liners; fans adore him and his spren Glys ("I love this boy" is a common refrain in fan art/discussion).
- **"What I see interferes with Odium's ability... Anyone close to me is difficult for him to read"** — his plot-crucial future-sight explanation.
- **Oathbringer moment:** Jasnah stays her Shardblade over him — "I know what you are. You're my cousin. Family, Renarin. Hold my hand. Run with me" (ch. 119). A hugely beloved beat (Jasnah speaking, about Renarin).
- **Wind and Truth:** the on-page confirmation of Renarin and Rlain's relationship is one of the most celebrated fan moments; his paradox line "truth is just the lie that happened" is frequently quoted.
- **Where to find it:** coppermind.net/wiki/Renarin_Kholin; Goodreads Oathbringer / Wind and Truth reader notes.

### Lift (Edgedancer) — comic relief with heart
- **"I don't talk to myself because I'm crazy." / "I do it because I'm awesome."** (*WoR* / *Edgedancer*) — her signature. **Big hitter** for Lift.
- **"Prepared to be awesome" / "awesomeness"** — her term for her Stormlight-from-food powers.
- **The Azish honorifics running gag** — "Your Pancakefulness" (*Edgedancer*).
- **"You get made immortal... But you still gotta piss like everyone else"** (*RoW*).
- **Where to find it:** goodreads.com/work/quotes/54097500-edgedancer; Coppermind Lift page; the Tor.com "Lift" excerpt.

### Teft (Windrunner) — the gruff heart of Bridge Four
- **Teaches Kaladin the First Ideal** in *The Way of Kings* (the "Living is harder than dying" gloss). **Big hitter** context-wise.
- **"I ain't grouchy. I just have a low threshold for stupidity."** (*WoR*).
- **His death and Dabbid's "Life. Before. Death."** (*RoW*, ~65%) — a gut-punch fans cite as one of the saddest moments in the series ("Confident, and somehow still full of hope, Teft died").
- **Where to find it:** Goodreads Way of Kings / RoW reader notes; Coppermind Teft page.

### Rock / Lunamor (Numuhukumakiaki'aialunamor) — Bridge Four's soul
- **"Airsick lowlanders"** — his catch-all disparagement of non-Horneaters; the single phrase most associated with Rock. **Big hitter** for Rock.
- **"Your soup is cold. That is idiom for 'You are all stupid.'"** (*Oathbringer*, when he picks up the Honorblade).
- **His refusal to fight, his role as cook/barber, and the "small cliff" joke** ("Nothing like falling from cliff to make a man out of a boy... It will be small cliff. I like you too much for large one").
- **Where to find it:** coppermind.net/wiki/Lunamor; Fandom "Rock" and "Airsick Lowlanders" pages.

### Lopen (Windrunner) — pure comedy
- **"It's not the number of hands that makes a man, but the number of cousins"** (*Wind and Truth*).
- **His arm regrowth** and the running gag of one-armed (then one-legged) Herdazian jokes (*WoR*, *Dawnshard*).
- **"If you're crazy, you're a good type... we all follow crazies all the time. Do it every day with lighteyes"** (*WoK*).
- **Teaching his spren Rua rude gestures**; the self-referential "the Lopen."
- **Where to find it:** coppermind.net/wiki/Lopen/Quotes; 17th Shard "The Lopen funny Quotes" thread; goodreads.com/quotes/tag/the-lopen.

### Navani Kholin (Bondsmith) — scholar-queen
- **"Journey before destination, you bastard."** (*Rhythm of War* ch. 110) — as she bonds the Sibling and stops Moash mid-strike. One of the most crowd-pleasing lines in RoW; standalone Goodreads quote #10556440. **Big hitter.**
- Her self-worth arc ("You are a scholar. You are a creator. You are enough") and her bond-through-trust with the Sibling ("I will not enslave you. I will listen. I will protect").
- **Where to find it:** coppermind.net/wiki/Navani_Kholin; Goodreads RoW reader notes (~92–94%).

### Moash / Vyre — the beloved-to-hate villain
- **His guilt-shedding creed** — in *Oathbringer* he tells himself to "let go," to give up his pain and accept he "can't be blamed" (a product of his culture) — the dark mirror of Dalinar's refusal to surrender his pain. Fans discuss this as his defining, chilling arc.
- **Killing Elhokar** (interrupting the king's own near-Radiant moment) and, as Vyre, **killing the Herald Jezrien** (*Oathbringer* end) — his most-referenced acts.
- He is the fandom's most-hated character; the ironic "Moash did nothing wrong" is a running meme.
- **Where to find it:** stormlightarchive.fandom.com/wiki/Moash; 17th Shard "Moash's views on Kaladin" thread; Goodreads Oathbringer reader notes.

### Syl (honorspren) — Kaladin's spren
- **"Humans don't make sense." / "...then you haven't been paying attention."** (*WoR*).
- **"That's what they all were, silly"** — her reply to Kaladin calling himself broken (*WoR*). **Big hitter** for the Kaladin/Syl relationship.
- **"Stretch forth thy hand!"** — at the WoR climax as she becomes his Shardblade.
- She originates "Honor is dead," which Kaladin completes.
- **Where to find it:** goodreads.com/quotes/tag/syl; Coppermind Syl page.

## Recommendations
1. **Build the app's spine around the Ideals first.** The First Ideal and the Windrunner oath ladder are the most-quoted and most thematically central lines; anchor the "oaths" feature on them, using coppermind.net/wiki/Knights_Radiant/Immortal_Words as the citation backbone (it has per-order, per-book, per-chapter references).
2. **Tag each quote with a "beloved tier."** Mark as top-tier: the First Ideal; Kaladin's Third and Fourth Ideals; "Honor is dead, but I'll see what I can do"; Dalinar's "You cannot have my pain"/"I am Unity" and "the next step"; Szeth's opening line; Navani's "Journey before destination, you bastard." These recur across all source types. Treat the humor lines (Lopen, Rock, Lift, Adolin banter) and character-specific creeds as mid-tier "fan favorites."
3. **For exact wording, link out rather than embed.** Point users to Coppermind "/Quotes" pages (best for attribution + chapter citations) and Goodreads tag/quote pages (best for community like-counts as a popularity proxy). This also keeps you clear of reproducing long copyrighted passages in-app.
4. **Add a dedicated humor lane.** Lopen, Rock, Lift, and Wit/Adolin banter form a distinct, heavily-loved category worth its own filter separate from the solemn oath quotes.
5. **Spoiler-gate Books 4–5.** Kaladin's Fourth Ideal, Szeth's Fifth Ideal, Kaladin-as-Herald, Dalinar's death, Teft's death, and Renarin/Rlain are major recent payoffs; hide them behind a spoiler toggle.

**Thresholds that would change these recommendations:** If you secure licensing or confirm fair use for short quotations, you can embed the <20-word "hero lines" directly instead of only linking out. If the app targets newcomers rather than veterans, de-emphasize the Book 4–5 payoffs and lead with Books 1–2 (Kaladin's rise, Szeth's opener, the boots scene, Dalinar's "priceless lives").

## Caveats
- **There is no official "most beloved quotes" ranking.** "Big hitter" designations here are inferred from convergence across Goodreads like-counts, Coppermind prominence, 17th Shard forum threads, TV Tropes, and roundup blogs — a reasonable proxy, not a poll. I was unable to pull a live r/Stormlight "favorite lines" megathread within budget, so Reddit consensus is represented indirectly through the aggregators; treat the tiering as directional.
- **Roundup blogs (snugfam, dalinar.com, bookroo, thyquotes, wisefamousquotes) sometimes misattribute or loosely paraphrase.** Verify against Coppermind or the primary text before publishing exact wording. Notably, the epigraph "As I fear not the child with a weapon he cannot lift, I will never fear the mind of a man who does not think" is an in-world *The Way of Kings* (Fourth Parable) epigraph that first appeared in *Words of Radiance* — it is not original spoken Wit dialogue, though it resurfaces in *Wind and Truth*.
- **Some famous "quotes" are exchanges or scenes, not single lines** (Jasnah's boots scene; the Dalinar/Kaladin "priceless lives" exchange; the WoR climax oath). Represent them as scenes-with-context rather than reducing them to one line.
- **Chapter numbers should be spot-checked against Coppermind** where I have given them (e.g., *Oathbringer* ch. 119 for "You cannot have my pain"; RoW ch. 110 for Navani; Szeth's Fifth Ideal ~ch. 133 of *Wind and Truth*) — these are corroborated across Goodreads notes and Coppermind but a single authoritative pass on the Coppermind "/Quotes" pages is worth doing before publishing.
- **Per the project's constraint, this guide deliberately describes rather than reproduces** long passages; the linked sources contain the verbatim text for fans who want it.
