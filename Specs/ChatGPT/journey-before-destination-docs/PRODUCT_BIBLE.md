# Product Bible
## Journey Before Destination

## What This App Is

Journey Before Destination is a Stormlight-inspired personal growth and reading companion.

It is for readers who connect with the deeper themes of Stormlight Archive and want to apply those ideas to their own life through reflection, personal oaths, and intentional action.

## What This App Is Not

- It is not a Coppermind clone.
- It is not just a habit tracker.
- It is not a generic productivity app.
- It is not a replacement for official Brandon Sanderson resources.
- It is not a social network.

## Product Philosophy

The app should feel serious and reflective, with just enough RPG flavor to make progression feel exciting.

The focus is not grinding points. The focus is taking the next step.

## Core Promise

Help Stormlight fans turn ideals into action.

## Core Pillars

### Growth
Users define personal oaths and connect them to real actions.

### Reflection
Users journal privately through prompts and freeform entries.

### Discovery
Users explore characters, Orders, books, and lore through the RadiantDex.

## Product Principles

1. Reflection over productivity.
2. Progress over perfection.
3. Private by default.
4. Stormlight-inspired, not Stormlight-dependent.
5. Actions matter more than streaks.
6. Spoiler safety matters.
7. Build small, but design for expansion.
8. Local-first for MVP.
9. Content should feel meaningful, not gimmicky.
10. The dashboard should always answer: what is my next step?

## Tone

The tone should feel:

- Reflective
- Intentional
- Encouraging
- Slightly epic
- Personal
- Calm

Avoid:

- Cheesy fantasy language
- Overly motivational hustle culture
- Fake therapy language
- Excessive gamification

## Core Tagline Options

- Turn ideals into action.
- Take the next step.
- A Stormlight-inspired companion for growth, reflection, and discovery.
- Your journey, your oaths, your next step.
